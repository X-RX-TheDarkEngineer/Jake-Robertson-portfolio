01 — About Me

Data Specialist.
AI Trainer. Builder.

A music producer’s ear for detail, applied to data and AI — listening closely, judging carefully, and building systems people can trust.

I’m Jake Robertson, an applied AI engineer and AI domain task architect based in New York, NY. I work in token-level model mechanics, context engineering, prompt design, Ground Truth creation, and multi-agent comparison — the human judgment layer that makes models actually useful, with a focus on source-verified, PII-safe, high-accuracy outputs.

My work through Mercor focuses on AI evaluation and task architecture: architecting complex, domain-specific training tasks for frontier models, authoring long-horizon agentic tasks with solution paths, rubrics, and curriculum guides, and creating Chain-of-Thought annotations and source-verified ground-truth datasets. In parallel, I’m building the engineering foundation to create those systems myself — Python, web development, and generative AI.
